# حدث طفلك

**Talk to Your Child** is a bilingual web application designed to help children express their emotions, communicate safely, and learn about personal safety through interactive and child-friendly activities.

The platform is designed for both **Arabic-speaking and English-speaking children**, providing an accessible environment where children can communicate in their preferred language while parents and trusted adults can gain helpful insights into their child's emotional well-being.

## 🎯 Project Goal

Children may sometimes find it difficult to talk directly about uncomfortable, confusing, or distressing experiences. The goal of this project is to provide children with alternative and engaging ways to express themselves while promoting awareness of safety, trusted relationships, and asking for help.

The platform is **not intended to diagnose abuse or determine whether harassment has occurred**. Instead, it acts as a supportive communication and early-awareness tool that can encourage healthy conversations between children and trusted adults.

## ✨ Main Features

### 💬 AI Child-Friendly Chat

An AI-powered conversational assistant allows children to communicate through age-appropriate conversations.

* Supports **Arabic and English**
* Uses simple and child-friendly language
* Encourages children to express their feelings
* Provides supportive and non-judgmental responses
* Can introduce educational conversations about safety and trusted adults
* Automatically communicates in the selected language

### 🎨 Interactive Drawing Board

Children can use a creative drawing space as another way to express themselves.

Features include:

* Freehand drawing
* Multiple colors
* Adjustable brush sizes
* Eraser
* Clear canvas
* Undo and redo
* Save drawings
* Share drawings with a trusted adult
* Option to submit drawings for AI-assisted interpretation

The AI should not treat a drawing as proof of abuse or harassment. Instead, it can use the drawing as a starting point for gentle, open-ended conversation.

### 🛡️ Child Safety & Harassment Awareness

The platform provides age-appropriate activities that help children understand:

* Safe and unsafe situations
* Trusted adults
* Personal boundaries
* How to ask for help
* What to do when someone makes them uncomfortable
* The difference between appropriate and inappropriate secrets
* How to communicate their feelings

### ❤️ Emotional Expression

Children can express how they feel through:

* Conversations
* Drawing
* Guided activities
* Stories and scenarios
* Emotion-based prompts

The system can identify **possible emotional patterns over time**, such as recurring expressions of fear, sadness, or discomfort. These patterns should be treated as signals for supportive conversation, **not as diagnoses or conclusions**.

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Parent & Trusted Adult Support

The platform can provide parents or trusted adults with supportive insights about the child's interactions.

For example:

> "Your child has been expressing feelings of sadness more frequently recently. Consider spending some time talking with them about how they are feeling."

The system should encourage communication rather than make accusations or unsupported conclusions.

## 🌍 Bilingual Design

The application supports:

**🇵🇸 Arabic**

* Full Arabic interface
* Right-to-left (RTL) layout
* Arabic AI conversations
* Arabic educational content

**🇬🇧 English**

* Full English interface
* Left-to-right (LTR) layout
* English AI conversations
* English educational content

The language can be switched without losing the child's current progress.

## 🔐 Privacy & Safety

Because the platform is designed for children, privacy and safety are core requirements.

The system should:

* Minimize the collection of personal information
* Avoid exposing children's private conversations unnecessarily
* Clearly distinguish AI-generated suggestions from professional advice
* Avoid diagnosing abuse or harassment
* Avoid making definitive conclusions from drawings or emotional patterns
* Encourage children to contact a trusted adult when they feel unsafe
* Provide appropriate escalation guidance when serious safety concerns are disclosed

## 🧠 AI-Assisted Communication

AI is used to support communication rather than replace parents, teachers, counselors, or child-protection professionals.

The AI can:

* Hold age-appropriate conversations
* Ask gentle follow-up questions
* Explain safety concepts
* Analyze drawings for broad emotional themes
* Generate supportive feedback
* Provide bilingual communication
* Help children identify trusted adults and appropriate ways to seek help

AI-generated interpretations should always be treated as **supportive observations rather than clinical or forensic assessments**.

## ⚠️ Important Disclaimer

This application is designed as a **communication, education, and early-awareness tool**. It is not a replacement for parents, caregivers, teachers, counselors, medical professionals, or child-protection services.

AI-generated responses and interpretations should not be considered professional, medical, psychological, or legal assessments.

If a child reports immediate danger, abuse, or harassment, the appropriate response should involve a trusted adult and, where necessary, qualified child-protection or emergency services.
